Why OmniAudit-GEO Wins #1

The Generative Shift

Over 60% of search traffic in 2026 is answered directly by conversational AI engines without traditional web clicks. Brands that fail AI discoverability become invisible.

The Dual Failure Mode

Off-site: AI bots are blocked in robots.txt or missing entity schemas. On-site: Referred visitors bounce in 4s due to lost contextual orientation.

Deterministic Rigor

Deterministic standard-library parsers use bounded fetching and offline regression fixtures without external API dependencies.
