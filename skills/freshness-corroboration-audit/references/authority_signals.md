# E-E-A-T & Authority Signals Reference Catalog

## Key Trust Markers for AI Search Retrieval
1. **Temporal Freshness:**
   * Updated copyright year matching the current calendar year.
   * Explicit `<meta property="article:modified_time">` or `dateModified` in Schema.org JSON-LD.
2. **Entity Transparency:**
   * Verifiable physical address, registered company name, and direct support email/phone.
   * Prominent links to Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Editorial Guidelines.
3. **Author Verification:**
   * Named authors with links to their LinkedIn or personal credentials on educational and technical content.
