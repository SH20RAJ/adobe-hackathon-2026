# On-Site Engagement & Cognitive Flow Heuristic Checklist

## 1. The 5-Second Orientation Rule
* When a visitor arrives via an AI referral link, can they answer within 5 seconds:
  1. *What is this product/service?*
  2. *Is this relevant to my AI search query?*
  3. *What action should I take next?*

## 2. Information Scent & Context Preservation
* Ensure the landing page directly mirrors the specific entity/topic cited by the AI assistant rather than forcing the user to re-navigate from a generic homepage.

## 3. Cognitive Friction Reducers
* Avoid wall-of-text paragraphs (> 4 lines).
* Maintain high text contrast against backgrounds.
* Keep primary CTA visible above the fold.
